# Code of conduct

This is a game for small children, and the people who work on it are mostly volunteers. Be kind to them and to each other.

- Say what you think of the work, and not of the person who did it.
- Keep everything you write here, in issues, in pull requests and in the code, fit for a child to read over a parent's shoulder.
- Do not post a child's name, a photo of a child or a saved world in this repository.
- Do not harass anyone, and do not share what someone told you in private.
- Assume that a person who asks a basic question has a reason to ask it.

The maintainers may edit or remove a comment, close an issue, or block a person who does not follow this. A decision comes with a reason when it can be given in public.

To report a problem with a person, message a maintainer privately on GitHub. A report is kept between the people who need to deal with it.
