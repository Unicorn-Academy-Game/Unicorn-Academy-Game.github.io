# Character board

Descriptors of the six unicorns as this game draws them, so a change to the renderer can be checked against what was meant. The
designs are a fan's reading of the characters of the show. No picture from the show is in this repository, and every picture in
the game is made by the renderer under `tools/art`.

Riders: Wildstar and Sophia, Leaf and Ava, Glacier and Layla, River and Isabel, Cinder and Valentina, Storm and Rory.

## Asymmetry

- The two flanks never carry mirrored markings. Wildstar has different sparkles on each side. Every other
  unicorn has its signature motif on the shoulder on one side and on the hip on the other, at different sizes and angles.
- Storm's face blaze is a head-space decal, so it follows every head pose and turn.
- Cinder's flame ornament and River's spiral are separate meshes on the cheek, so they read from the side and the front.

## Shared rig

The six share one rig and gait. They differ in coat, markings, hair style and length, halter parts, horn length and
turns, eye shape and build factors (bulk, neck length, head scale).

## Known gap

Body silhouettes are still close to each other. At phone size, color, markings, hair and halter carry identity. Larger
silhouette differences (Glacier taller and finer, Cinder sturdier) are a candidate for a polish pass.
